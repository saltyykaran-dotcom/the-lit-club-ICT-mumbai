Our 2026–27 theme

Theme: Threshold

The in-between is not a pause. It is where possibility begins.

This year, our theme revolves around a resonant idea: Threshold.

College is about to become a space stuck between who we are back home and who we strive to become. Jumping off of last year’s theme, Nostalgia, Threshold is that exact liminal space between places where we get messy, start thinking on our feet, express our hearts out and figure out what comes next for us.

Through open-mics, spirited debates, engrossing quizzes and so much more, we tickle that creative side of yours that may have been hidden for a long time. This year is dedicated to unique stories, amazing people and choices that shape us.

The Literary Club isn't looking for finished, polished narratives. We want the drafts. We want the unrefined, authentic stories of what it feels like to stand right on the edge of your own future—nervous, hopeful, and entirely alive to the possibilities of what comes next.

A thought to carry
“We want the drafts. We want the unrefined, authentic stories.”

What we do

Providing a stage for the unknown called the in-between.

For every voice, every idea, every first draft.

Welcome to the Literary Club of ICT Mumbai, where top engineering minds come together into a vibrant and creative epicentre of imagination, knowledge, words and expression.

Whether you are an insatiable reader who gets lost in classic literature, a passionate poet looking for a stage to share your inspired verses, an aspiring writer carefully crafting your first story, or simply someone who loves spirited debates on philosophy and culture, this is your community.

Our club is meant to break the monotony of equations and derivations by offering a welcoming, judgment-free space to explore books, poetry, creative writing, quizzing, and expressive storytelling.

Step away from the screen, open a page, and join us as we turn ordinary campus days into unforgettable chapters of shared stories and ideas.

On the club calendar

Make a little noise.

From quick-fire quizzes to conversations that stay with you, there is a stage for every kind of curious mind.

01 / Spirit Days

A high-energy start to college life

QUIZ · TEAMWORK · FUN

Game of Games

A thrilling multi-round quiz that challenges participants across a unique range of genres and niches, mixing extreme intellect and high-energy fun with a wave of enjoyment.

ANONYMOUS EXPRESSION

Yours Anonymously

A quiet and open space for expression where notes, confessions, letters, and unsent thoughts find a voice. Share your story without the weight of identity.

POETRY · SPOKEN WORD

Open-Mic

An intimate platform for spoken word, poetry, storytelling, and acoustic performances. No judgment, no strict rules—just an open stage for sharing raw, honest pieces of yourself.

02 / Collaboration

Manzar × Lit Club

CULTURE MEETS CROWD INSTINCT

Family Feud

A dashing collaboration with our biggest cultural club brings you an event beloved all over the globe: Family Feud. Adapted from the classic game show, teams go head-to-head to guess the most popular answers to quirky survey questions about campus life, pop culture, and daily student habits. It tests your ability to read the room and think like the crowd while finding exciting answers within a time limit.

03 / Expression

Sharp intellect, word mastery & stage presence

UNCONVENTIONAL TALENT

ICT’s got Latent

The ultimate platform for unconventional, hidden, or hilarious talents. From stand-up comedy and beatboxing to quirky literary acts, if you have something unique to show, this stage is yours.

VOCABULARY · PRECISION

Spell Bee

A classic test of vocabulary, linguistic precision, and composure under pressure. Participants face obscure words, tricky etymologies, and fast-paced rounds to claim the title of ultimate word masters.

LOGIC · RHETORIC · IDEAS

Lit Club Debate

A battlefield of ideas where logic, rhetoric, and persuasion meet. Debaters clash over contemporary topics, ethics, and philosophy—learning to defend an argument while dissecting opposing points of view.

04 / Flagship Event

ICT Model United Nations

DIPLOMACY · NEGOTIATION

ICT Model United Nations

One of the Literary Club’s biggest flagship events, featuring delegates stepping into the shoes of global diplomats to simulate real-world UN committees. Delegates negotiate, policies are drafted, and participants navigate complex international geopolitics to resolve pressing global crisis scenarios.

05 / Democracy in action

Lit Club Lok Sabha

PARLIAMENT · POLICY · GOVERNANCE

Lit Club Lok Sabha

A dynamic simulation of the Indian parliament where participants step into the roles of ministers and opposition leaders. Expect fiery debates, legislative bill discussions, points of order, and deep dives into domestic policy and governance—perfectly recreating the high-tension, high-stakes conversations and debates that forge our country’s future.
